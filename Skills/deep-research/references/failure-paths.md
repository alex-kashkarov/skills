# Failure Paths

## Research Question Will Not Converge

Trigger: multiple rounds still leave the question broad or unstable.

Recovery:

1. Summarize the user's expressed interests.
2. Offer 2-3 candidate research questions.
3. Ask the user to choose, combine, or reject them.
4. If still unclear, switch to `lit-review` to map the field before committing.

## Insufficient Literature

Trigger: fewer than 3 usable sources after normal search.

Recovery:

- Expand keywords and synonyms.
- Search adjacent disciplines.
- Extend date range.
- Add official statistics, reports, working papers, or grey literature.
- Reframe as exploratory if the evidence remains sparse.

## Plan Not Approved

Trigger: the user rejects or adjusts the proposed plan at the approval gate.

Recovery:

- Identify the exact rejected part: scope, sub-question, source route, tier, or output shape.
- Revise the plan once using the user's correction.
- Ask for approval again.
- If the second plan is still rejected, stop and ask the user for the missing decision instead of guessing.

## Weak Source Quality

Trigger: all sources grade `C` or lower.

Recovery:

- Downgrade confidence.
- Label findings preliminary.
- Distinguish what is known from what is plausible.
- Recommend primary research or better data collection.

## Method Does Not Fit Question

Trigger: the proposed evidence cannot answer the research question.

Recovery:

- Name the mismatch directly.
- Offer alternative methods or revised questions.
- Do not proceed to synthesis until the question/evidence fit is repaired.

## Critical Contradiction

Trigger: strong evidence directly challenges the likely conclusion.

Recovery:

- Surface the contradiction in the answer.
- Compare source quality.
- Narrow, reverse, or qualify the conclusion.

## Verification Failure

Trigger: source existence or claim support cannot be confirmed.

Recovery:

- Remove the source.
- Replace it with a verified source.
- If no replacement exists, mark the claim unverifiable.

## User Wants Unsupported Certainty

Trigger: user asks for a definitive claim that evidence cannot support.

Recovery:

- Refuse the certainty, not the task.
- Give the strongest evidence-bounded conclusion.
- State what evidence would be needed to raise confidence.
